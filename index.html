Cuban Cocktail: Classic Mojito

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum (view photo)
  • 1 fresh lime, cut into wedges (view photo)
  • 6 to 8 fresh mint leaves (view photo)
  • 2 teaspoons white sugar (view photo)
  • 1/2 cup soda water (view photo)
  • 1 cup crushed ice (view photo)

Instructions

  1. Place the fresh mint leaves and one lime wedge into a sturdy highball glass.
  2. Use a muddler to crush the mint and lime together to release the mint oils and lime juice.
  3. Add two more lime wedges and the white sugar, then muddle again without shredding the leaves.
  4. Fill the glass almost to the top with crushed ice.
  5. Pour the white rum over the ice and top off the glass with soda water.
  6. Stir gently, garnish with a mint sprig, and serve with a straw.
